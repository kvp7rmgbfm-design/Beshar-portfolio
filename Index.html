Places I've Visited

Saudi Arabia has given me memories I'll carry for a lifetime. Two cities stand out above the rest.

🌊

Jeddah

City by the Red Sea · Saudi Arabia

Jeddah is alive with energy. The coastline stretches along the Red Sea, and the humid breeze carries something warm and welcoming. I love walking along the waterfront, watching waves roll in, and feeling completely at ease.

The city's restaurants and shopping areas draw visitors from across the globe — and one place I never miss is Albaik, whose fried chicken has become a genuine piece of Saudi culture.

🏖️ Red Sea Beach 🍗 Albaik 🛍️ Shopping Districts 🌅 Waterfront Walks
🕌

Makkah

Holiest City in Islam · Saudi Arabia

Visiting Makkah feels different from anywhere else on Earth. Millions of Muslims arrive from every corner of the world, yet the atmosphere is one of peace, unity, and profound respect. It always deepens my connection to my faith.

The towering modern buildings surrounding the holy area are breathtaking — especially at night, when city lights transform the skyline into something truly majestic. Having family there makes every visit even more special.

🙏 Spiritual Peace 🏙️ Stunning Architecture 🌍 Global Unity 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Time
